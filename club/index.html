Small Print

A few things to keep in mind

Terms & Conditions

  • Your club will receive a cheque in July for 15% of pre-tax sales made with its coupon code.
  • Credit cards must be registered to a Nova Scotia address. Delivery must also be within Nova Scotia.
  • Free delivery still applies; a delivery fee applies for smaller orders.
  • For delivery, someone 19 or older must be home and present valid government-issued photo ID. If no one is available, the order returns to the store and redelivery incurs an additional charge.
  • Pick-up orders must be collected within 48 hours — stores have limited on-site storage.
  • Harvest Wines will not sell or deliver alcohol to anyone under the age of 19.
  • Online sales only. Not valid in-store. Applies to all products including sale items.
  • You do not need to be a Rotarian to use your club's code — share it freely with Nova Scotia friends, family, and neighbours!
